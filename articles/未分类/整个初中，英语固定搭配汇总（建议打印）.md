很多初中的孩子英语丢分，并不是单词掌握的不好，而是固定搭配记不住。
**而单选题、完形填空、写作，到处都在考词语固定搭配。记住以下初中阶段的搭配词，再也不用担心成绩上不去了~非常的重要！**

**一、七年级必背固定搭配（基础必考）**
**1. be afraid of 害怕**
 She is afraid of snakes. 她害怕蛇。
**2. be busy with 忙于**
 My mom is busy with housework. 妈妈忙于家务。
**3. be from 来自**
 He is from Canada. 他来自加拿大。
**4. be good at 擅长**
 Tom is good at playing basketball. 汤姆擅长打篮球。
**5. be good for 对……有益**
 Milk is good for our health. 牛奶对我们的健康有益。
**6. be good with 善于应付；和……相处融洽**
 The teacher is good with kids. 这位老师很擅长和孩子相处。
**7. be interested in 对……感兴趣**
 I am interested in history. 我对历史感兴趣。
**8. be late for 迟到**
 Don’t be late for school. 上学不要迟到。
**9. be ready for 为……做准备**
 We are ready for the exam. 我们为考试做好准备。
**10. be ready to do 准备做某事**
 She is ready to help others. 她准备好帮助别人。
**11. borrow … from 从……借入**
 I borrow books from the library. 我从图书馆借书。
**12. enjoy doing 喜欢做某事**
 They enjoy swimming in summer. 他们夏天喜欢游泳。
**13. finish doing 完成做某事**
 I finish doing my homework at 8. 我八点写完作业。
**14. help sb do sth / help sb with sth 帮助某人做某事**
 He helps me (to) learn English. / He helps me with my English. 他帮我学英语。
**15. how about doing / what about doing 做……怎么样**
 What about going for a walk? 出去散步怎么样？
**16. It takes sb some time to do 花费某人多长时间做某事**
 It takes me 30 minutes to go to school. 我上学要花30分钟。
**17. It's time to do / for sth 该做……了**
 It’s time to have class. / It’s time for class. 该上课了。
**18. let sb do sth 让某人做某事**
 Let him clean the room. 让他打扫房间。
**19. lend … to 把……借给**
 I lend my pen to Lily. 我把笔借给莉莉。
**20. like doing / to do 喜欢做某事**
 She likes reading / to read books. 她喜欢看书。
**21. look forward to doing 期待做某事（to是介词，后接doing）**
 We look forward to meeting you. 我们期待见到你。
**22. mind doing 介意做某事**
 Do you mind opening the window? 你介意开窗吗？
**23. pay ... for 为……付款**
 I pay 20 yuan for the bag. 我花20元买这个包。
**24. practice doing 练习做某事**
 He practices speaking English every day. 他每天练习说英语。
**25. provide sb with sth / provide sth for sb 向某人提供某物**
 The school provides students with books. = The school provides books for students. 学校给学生提供书籍。
**26. spend ... on sth / (in) doing 花费（时间/金钱）在……**
 I spend one hour on homework / (in) doing homework. 我花一小时写作业。
**27. thanks for doing 感谢做某事**
 Thanks for helping me. 谢谢你帮我。
**28. want to do 想要做某事**
 They want to visit the museum. 他们想去参观博物馆。
**29. would like to do 想要做某事**
 I would like to drink some water. 我想喝点水。
**二、八年级必背固定搭配（高频拉分）**
**1. be angry with sb 生某人的气**
 My dad is angry with me. 爸爸生我的气。
**2. be away from 远离**
 Keep away from fire. 远离火源。
**3. be covered with 被……覆盖**
 The mountain is covered with snow. 山上被白雪覆盖。
**4. be different from 与……不同**
 My bag is different from yours. 我的包和你的不一样。
**5. be excited about 对……感到兴奋**
 She is excited about the trip. 她对这次旅行很兴奋。
**6. be famous for 因……闻名**
 China is famous for the Great Wall. 中国因长城闻名。
**7. be famous as 作为……闻名**
 He is famous as a writer. 他作为一名作家而出名。
**8. be filled with 充满**
 The box is filled with books. 箱子里装满书。
**9. be full of 充满**
 The box is full of books. 箱子里装满书。
**10. be harmful to 对……有害**
 Smoking is harmful to health. 吸烟有害健康。
**11. be helpful to 对……有帮助**
 Reading is helpful to us. 阅读对我们有益。
**12. be in danger 处于危险中**
 The animal is in danger. 这种动物处于危险之中。
**13. be in need 需要帮助**
 We should help people in need. 我们应当帮助有需要的人。
**14. be in trouble 处于困境**
 If you are in trouble, call me. 如果你遇到麻烦，可以打电话给我。
**15. be necessary for 对……是必要的**
 Sleep is necessary for kids. 睡眠对孩子必不可少。
**16. be nervous about 对……感到紧张**
 I’m nervous about the test. 这次考试让我很紧张。
**17. be out of 从……出来；缺乏**
 We are out of water. 我们的水用完了。
**18. be pleased with 对……满意**
 My teacher is pleased with my work. 老师对我的作业满意。
**19. be popular with 受……欢迎**
 This song is popular with students. 这首歌很受学生欢迎。
**20. be proud of 为……骄傲**
 I am proud of my country. 我为我的祖国骄傲。
**21. be responsible for 对……负责**
 You are responsible for your choice. 你要为自己的选择负责。
**22. be satisfied with 对……满意**
 He is satisfied with his new bike. 他对新自行车很满意。
**23. be similar to 与……相似**
 Her dress is similar to mine. 她的裙子和我的很像。
**24. be sorry for 为……抱歉**
 He is sorry for his mistake. 他为自己的错误感到抱歉。
**25. be strict with sb / be strict about sth 对某人严格 / 对某事严格**
 Parents are strict with me. We must be strict about our studies. 父母对我严格，我们必须认真对待学业。
**26. be surprised at 对……惊讶**
 I’m surprised at the news. 听到这个消息我很惊讶。
**27. be thankful to 对……感激**
 I’m thankful to my teachers. 我很感激我的老师们。
**28. be used to doing 习惯于（to是介词）**
 He is used to getting up early. 他习惯早起。
**29. be used to do 被用来做**
 Knives are used to cut things. 刀被用来切东西。
**30. be useful to 对……有用**
 A dictionary is useful to students. 词典对学生有用。
**31. be worried about 担心**
 She is worried about her exam. 她担心考试。
**32. be the same as 与……相同**
 Your pen is the same as his. 你的钢笔和他的一样。
**33. as…as… 和……一样**
 Tom runs as fast as Jack. 汤姆跑得和杰克一样快。
**34. not as/so…as… 不如……**
 I am not so tall as you. 我没有你高。
**35. not…until… 直到……才**
 I didn’t go to bed until 10. 我直到十点才睡觉。
**36. prefer A to B 比起B更喜欢A**
 I prefer tea to coffee. 比起咖啡我更喜欢茶。
**37. prefer doing to doing 比起做B更喜欢做A**
 I prefer reading to watching TV. 比起看电视我更喜欢阅读。
**38. prefer to do rather than do 宁愿做……而不愿做**
 She prefers to walk rather than take a bus. 她宁愿走路也不坐公交。
**39. so…that… 如此……以至于（so+形容词/副词）**
 He is so tired that he can’t walk. 他太累了，走不动路。
**40. such…that… 如此……以至于（such+名词短语）**
 It is such a good book that I read twice. 这本书太好了，我读了两遍。
**41. take pride in 以……为傲**
 I take pride in my progress. 我为自己的进步感到自豪。
**42. the +比较级，the +比较级 越……，就越……**
 The more you read, the more you know. 读得越多，懂得越多。
**43. too…to… 太……而不能**
 The box is too heavy to carry. 箱子太重搬不动。
**44. used to do 过去常常（现在不再这样）**
 I used to live here. 我以前住在这里。
**45. would rather do than do 宁愿做……而不做**
 I would rather stay home than go out. 我宁愿待在家里也不外出。
**三、九年级必背固定搭配（中考高频）**
**1. as far as 就……而言**
 As far as I know, he is honest. 据我所知，他很诚实。
**2. as long as 只要**
 You can pass as long as you work hard. 只要努力你就能通过。
**3. as soon as 一……就……**
 I will call you as soon as I arrive. 我一到就给你打电话。
**4. as well as 也；和（就远原则）**
 Tom as well as his sister likes music. 汤姆和他妹妹都喜欢音乐。
**5. at first 起初**
 At first I didn’t like English. 起初我不喜欢英语。
**6. at last 最后**
 At last, we won the game. 最后我们赢得比赛。
**7. at least 至少**
 Sleep at least 8 hours. 至少睡8小时。
**8. at most 最多**
 Wait at most ten minutes. 最多等十分钟。
**9. at once 立刻**
 Leave here at once. 立刻离开这里。
**10. at present 目前**
 At present, he works in a hospital. 目前他在医院工作。
**11. at the end of 在……末尾（时间/地点）**
 At the end of the road is a shop. 在路的尽头有一家商店。
**12. at times 有时**
 I go hiking at times. 我有时去徒步。
**13. both…and… ……和……两者都**
 Both you and I like sports. 你和我都喜欢运动。
**14. by the end of 到……结束时（+过去时间，主句用过去完成时）**
 By the end of last year, we had learned 2000 words. 到去年年底，我们已经学了2000个单词。
**15. by the time 到……的时候**
 By the time I got home, Mom had cooked dinner. 我到家的时候妈妈已经做好晚饭。
**16. either…or… 要么……要么……（就近原则）**
 Either you or he is wrong. 不是你错就是他错。
**17. enough to… 足够……去做**
 He is old enough to go to school. 他年龄够大，可以上学。
**18. in a word 总之**
 In a word, we should study hard. 总之我们要努力学习。
**19. in common 共有；共同**
 We have something in common. 我们有共同点。
**20. in danger 处于危险**
 Many wild animals are in danger. 很多野生动物处于危险之中。
**21. in fact 事实上**
 In fact, I don’t know him. 事实上，我不认识他。
**22. in general 一般来说**
 In general, girls like soft music. 一般来说女孩喜欢轻音乐。
